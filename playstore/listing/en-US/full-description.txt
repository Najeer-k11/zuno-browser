Zuno is a private, beautiful web browser that blocks ads and trackers before they load, so pages open faster, cleaner and calmer.

Most Android browsers are Chrome underneath. Zuno is different: it runs on Gecko, the independent open-source engine behind Firefox, wrapped in a Material You design that feels at home on your phone.

ZUNO SHIELD: BLOCK ADS & TRACKERS
• Blocks ads, trackers, cryptominers and fingerprinting scripts
• Auto-rejects annoying cookie consent pop-ups
• HTTPS-only mode and Global Privacy Control
• See how many ads and trackers were blocked on every page
• Pause blocking for any site with one tap
• Want more? Add uBlock Origin in one tap from Settings

TABS, THE SMARTER WAY
• Vertical tab sidebar instead of a crowded tab grid
• Workspaces for Personal, Work, Study... swipe to switch
• Pin your favourite sites to a quick grid in every workspace
• Swipe the address bar to jump between tabs

BACKGROUND PLAY
• Keep music and videos playing when you leave the app or lock your screen
• Play, pause and skip from your notifications and lock screen
• Works with music and video sites like YouTube and YouTube Music in the browser

PRIVATE MODE THAT MEANS IT
• A separate space with its own tabs and its own look
• No history, no saved searches
• Cookies and site data are wiped when you close private tabs

MATERIAL YOU, EXPRESSIVE DESIGN
• Colours that follow your wallpaper, or pick a palette
• Light, dark and pure-black (AMOLED) themes
• Compact mode: the toolbar slides away as you scroll, giving pages the full screen
• Smooth, playful animations throughout

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No account, no sign-in
• No analytics, no ads, no tracking by us
• Your tabs, history and settings stay on your phone

MORE
• Choose your search engine: DuckDuckGo, Startpage, Brave Search, Ecosia, Google or Bing
• Desktop site mode, find in page, downloads and secure-connection details for every site
• Clear cookies and data for one site, or everything

Zuno Browser is built with Mozilla GeckoView. Zuno is an independent app and is not affiliated with Mozilla.
