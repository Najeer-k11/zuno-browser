The short version

Zuno Browser does not collect, sell, rent or share your personal information. We do not run servers that receive your browsing data, and the app contains no advertising or analytics libraries. Everything Zuno remembers about you, such as open tabs, history and settings, stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

What is stored on your device

  • Tabs and workspaces, including pinned tabs and the page history inside each tab, so they can be restored when you reopen the app.
  • Browsing history (page addresses and titles), used only for address-bar suggestions. You can clear it in Settings → Data.
  • Settings such as theme, search engine, ad-blocking choices and the list of sites where you paused blocking.
  • Website data such as cookies, cache and site storage, kept by the browser engine like any web browser. You can clear it in Settings → Data or per site from the shield in the address bar.
  • A counter of how many ads and trackers were blocked, shown on the home page.

None of this is uploaded anywhere by Zuno. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Private mode

Tabs opened in private mode are never saved to disk and never added to your history. When you close your private tabs, the browser engine deletes their cookies, cache and site data.

When Zuno connects to the internet

Like any browser, Zuno connects to the websites you visit. Beyond that, it can contact these services:

ServiceWhy
Your chosen search engine (DuckDuckGo by default)To show results for what you type in the address bar. Their own privacy policy applies to your searches.
Mozilla services used by the GeckoView engineTo keep tracking-protection lists, cookie-banner rules and Safe Browsing data up to date, so known trackers and dangerous sites can be blocked. These requests do not include your browsing history.
addons.mozilla.orgOnly if you choose to install an extension such as uBlock Origin from Settings.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state, to load web pages.
  • Notifications, to show playback controls when music or video keeps playing in the background. Optional; Zuno works without it.
  • Foreground service (media playback), to keep audio playing when you leave the app. Turn it off in Settings → Browsing → Background play.
  • Files you pick: when a website asks you to upload a file, Zuno only reads the file you choose in Android's file picker. Downloads are saved to your Downloads folder.

Websites may ask for access to your camera, microphone or location. Zuno does not grant these to websites.

Ad and tracker blocking

Zuno Shield blocks ads and trackers on your device using built-in lists. Deciding what to block happens entirely on your phone. Nothing about the pages you visit is sent to us or to anyone else for this.

Children

Zuno is a general-purpose web browser and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Third-party websites

Websites you visit with Zuno have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover. Zuno reduces what they can track, but it cannot control what a site does with information you give it directly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
